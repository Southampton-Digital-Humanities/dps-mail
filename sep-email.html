Upcoming Workshop
Foundations of Digital Preservation: Integrity Checking, Format Migration, and Workflows (Online)
Following the success of our Mastering Metadata workshops, we are launching a new exciting workshop. This is your digital preservation essentials course; a hands-on, online workshop designed to introduce the core concepts, tools, and workflows related to the preservation of digital assets. Learn to identify and migrate file formats, create safe data transfers, and ensure long-term access through integrity checks. Through this workshop, you will earn experience with key open-source and industry-standard software such as DROID, Siegfried, Teracopy, and standards such as BagIt. Registrations are open. Do not miss your chance!
